# The Borders of Greece, 1821 – today

An atlas of the land held by Greece or its armed forces, day by day, from the revolution of 1821 to today: the borders recognized by
treaty, the lands held without recognition (the revolution's districts, Smyrna and Asia Minor, Eastern Thrace, Northern Epirus), the treaty awards that were never carried out (the 1830 line, Berlin 1880, Sèvres) and the occupations of Greek territory.
372 records (333 dated changes of control and 39 notes), each with its basis, its sources and its doubts.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/borders-of-greece/

Files: `index.html` (the page, with the records), `geo.json` (the shapes), `relief.jpg` and `land.png` (the map), `img/` and `thumb/` (stills
of the film), `dl/` (downloads: GeoJSON, CSV, xlsx, JSON, the kit).
